02 • Introduction

The Problem / Background

Fare transparency remains a persistent challenge in local transportation when passengers lack immediate, objective ways to verify their expected fare.

The Current Passenger Journey

1. Trip Initiation

Passenger boards local tricycle

2. Fare Request

Driver issues fare charge upon arrival

3. Verification Gap

No accessible digital reference to verify accuracy

4. Accountability Issue

Trip documentation and reporting are hindered

The Core Need

A unified mechanism providing accessible fare information, trip documentation, and reporting support.

10 • Measurement

Evaluation Framework

Four quantitative metrics will be measured using controlled system testing with the validated Agoo fare matrix as reference.

Computation Latency

CL (in ms)

CL = Tfare display − Tcomputation initiation

Measured from destination confirmation to fare display, across multiple origin-destination pairs.

Location Update Latency

RLUL (in s)

RLUL = Tdisplayed − Treceived

Measured from GPS reading availability to successful display during actual route tracking.

Fare Computation Accuracy

FCA (in %)

FCA = (Ncorrect ÷ Ntotal) × 100

GeoFare fare compared vs. validated fare matrix. Fare error also computed as % deviation.

Application Crash Rate

ACR (in %)

ACR = (Ncrashed ÷ Nsessions) × 100

Records unexpected termination, freezing, or failure during repeated core-function test sessions.

11 • Research Integrity

Ethical Considerations

The study observes core ethical protocols to protect all respondents and uphold research integrity.

Informed Consent

All respondents will be informed of the study's purpose before participation. Participation is entirely voluntary.

Confidentiality & Data Privacy

All data handled per the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Information used solely for academic purposes.

Institutional Endorsement

Official approval from the LGU Agoo via a commitment letter will be secured before any data-gathering activity.

Non-Maleficence

No harm, inconvenience, or undue burden will come to respondents or drivers incidentally captured during testing.
