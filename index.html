01 • Introduction

Background of the Study

Fare transparency remains a persistent challenge in local transportation when passengers lack immediate, objective ways to verify their expected fare.

The Current Passenger Journey

1. Trip Initiation

Passenger boards local tricycle

2. Fare Request

Driver issues fare charge upon arrival

3. Verification Gap

No accessible digital reference to verify accuracy

4. Accountability Issue

Trip documentation and reporting are hindered

The Core Need

A unified mechanism providing accessible fare information, trip documentation, and reporting support.

12 • Measurement

Evaluation Framework

Four quantitative metrics measured through controlled testing against the validated Agoo fare matrix.

Computation Latency

CL

Formula

CL = Tfare display − Tcompute init

From destination confirmation to fare display across multiple OD pairs.

Target: ≤ 200 ms

Location Update Latency

RLUL

Formula

RLUL = Tdisplayed − Treceived

From GPS reading availability to successful display during route tracking.

Target: ≤ 1.2 s

Fare Accuracy

FCA

Formula

FCA = (Ncorrect ÷ Ntotal) × 100

System-generated fares compared vs. the validated Agoo fare matrix.

Reference: LGU Fare Matrix

Application Crash Rate

ACR

Formula

ACR = (Ncrashed ÷ Nsessions) × 100

Unexpected termination, freezing, or failure during repeated test sessions.

Target: 0%
